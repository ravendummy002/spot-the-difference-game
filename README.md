Three Evenings is a spot-the-difference game that runs offline in a desktop browser. Two near-identical pictures sit side by side — a fishing harbour at dawn, a lantern market at night, and a moonlit garden pond — and you click the differences hiding in them. Thirty differences across three sets of rising difficulty, a countdown timer that bites (wrong clicks cost 5 seconds), hints that pulse over a hidden difference, and a score that rewards fast, careful eyes. Every scene is drawn with code; no image, sound or font is loaded from the network.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. Click a difference on either picture to find it, press H for a hint, M to mute, R to restart the set, Enter to continue.
